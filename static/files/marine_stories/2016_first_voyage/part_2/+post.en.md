_2016.09.27-28_ Under Taiwan there was an incredible typhoon, so we hid near this islet and waited for the weather to improve. Our vessel rolled about 20 degrees, but we cadets still stood watch on deck, because in this region theft of mooring ropes happens.

##### 2016.10.06

We reached Singapore through storms to take on fuel and provisions. Since a new captain came to us during the repair, he approved the provision order, adding a bunch of milk and cookies. He himself was rather portly, but a kind-hearted man.

##### 2016.11.05

A little more time passed, and we reached the port of Nador in Morocco. There was very little left!

We brought Chinese garbage to the Moroccans, but they refused to unload it due to bad windy weather. The chief mate took responsibility and we unloaded it ourselves.
