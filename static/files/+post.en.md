This will be a small blog about my life, thoughts that have visited and continue to visit me, as well as topics that I found interesting to discuss at some point.

Many of the posts will be compilations from my Telegram channels: [Katsu Travel Diary](https://t.me/katsu_travel) and [Katsu Nikki](https://t.me/katsu_diary), but over time, the blog will be filled with new content.

In general, about me. I'm quite a typical person for the modern world. I studied to become a navigator in the merchant navy and completed several sea voyages as a navigator. However, in 2021, I changed my profession and embarked on conquering the IT field in backend development. Up until now, I have mastered Ruby on Rails, TypeScript, Node.js, React, Svelte, and many other technologies, which makes me incredibly happy.

I'm also passionate about Japanese culture, anime, and rhythm games like BeatSaber or Project Diva.

More details will be shared in the upcoming posts.
