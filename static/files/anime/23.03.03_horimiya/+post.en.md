Today, I finished watching Horimiya - a very warm and cute anime.

Hori Kyoko is a beauty and an excellent student, while Miyamura Izumi is a rather gloomy loner. What could unite them? The fact that they are not at all what they seem at first glance!

Hori-san is very family-oriented and has to take care of her little brother at home while her mother works late, and Miyamura-kun is a cool handsome guy with lots of piercings and tattoos that he hides from everyone at school. They accidentally reveal their true selves to each other. Hori's little brother got lost and Izumi brought him home.

At first, Izumi came to play with the little brother at his request, but in the end, meeting after meeting, the main characters realized that they were no longer just classmates and not just friends, and they confessed their feelings to each other.

The whole series is filled with warm and almost unrealistic relationships that you won't find in real life. There are also several side romantic stories between Hori and Miyamura's classmates.

I really liked this anime, even though I had read the manga it was adapted from before.
