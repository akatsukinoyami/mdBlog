Recently, I started rewatching some relatively old anime, and it reminded me of a cool anime called "Hyouka".

The story is about a lazy guy who "won't do what can be left undone, but if something needs to be done, he'll do it quickly" - Oreki Houtarou, and a very unusual girl from the local ancient family - Chitanda Eru.

Her magical phrase "I want to know!" and enchanting purple eyes make our main character activate his detective abilities and solve small school mysteries one after another.

In the end, of course, they fall in love with each other.

For the year 2012, the animation in this anime is simply incredible, surpassing modern series. Especially the character designs. And the characters themselves turned out to be very lifelike.

At first, I even considered dropping it, but I couldn't, even though I've dropped a lot of series lately. And that means something.

I recommend you to pay attention to it.
