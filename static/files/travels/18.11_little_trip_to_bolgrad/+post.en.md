Cold November Odessa. A good friend suggested we take a trip to a small town in the south of our region—Bolhrad. It’s logical to assume that, given the name, many Bulgarians have lived there for ages.  
“Why not?” I thought and agreed. Who could have known that Bolhrad would be hosting a wine festival!?
