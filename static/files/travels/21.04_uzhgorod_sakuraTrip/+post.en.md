After what I then thought was a difficult contract, I wanted to relax and unwind, so my friends and I agreed to go to Uzhhorod to see the cherry blossoms blooming everywhere there.

Since it was a much longer trip for me from Odesa than for my friends from Kyiv and Lviv, I arrived a day early and booked a triple room in a cool hostel right in the city center.
Besides, Uzhhorod is a pretty small city, so the whole town is like Odesa’s downtown)
