We left Lviv by bus and headed to Prague via Poland, Kraków, as indicated on it.  
Lviv – Kraków – Bohumín (transfer to the train) – Prague

##### 2018.07.11 23:25

Meanwhile, Katsu is approaching the Polish–Ukrainian border.

##### 2018.07.12

###### 00:05

We are standing in line, waiting our turn to go through border control.

###### 02:45

We stood here for three hours, and only now have we reached Ukrainian customs…

###### 06:20

And here, Katsu’s foot has set foot on European soil for the first time (we’ll skip the several-hour wait opposite the prison in England).  
Well, you can feel the difference in the roads immediately—my backside sends respect to the road builders.  
Wow, instead of power line pylons, huge wind turbines stand in the fields.  
A fog has descended over Poland; the sky is completely overcast, a weather Katsu really likes.  
Because we spent so long at the border, we missed the train on our ticket and will take the next one.  
We are approaching Katowice, and from there it’s not far to Bohumín (that’s near Ostrava).

###### 12:00

So, Katsu has boarded the train—Leo Express held a dedicated train just for our bus.  
This train is like a spaceship compared to our electric local trains; it feels like riding in a $25K+ car.  
The train is as quiet as modern electric cars—no chug-chug—on one hand that’s awesome, but on the other it lacks soul.  
I don’t know how fast it’s going—GPS isn’t working—but it feels at least 200 km/h.  
In fact, it’s 260–270 km/h. Only a plane is faster 😂

###### 15:15

Hello, long-awaited Prague! Katsu has arrived!

###### 15:55

And now, Katsu is at KFC for the first time—everyone told him about this place, and here he is in person.  
Still, it’s hard to manage here without knowing the language, hehe.
