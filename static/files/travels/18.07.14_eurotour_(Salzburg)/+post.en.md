##### 2018.07.15

We had an interesting night) Until midnight, we were at an Austrian equivalent of McDonald’s—Burgerista—a truly amazing place where they grill the burgers right in front of you, and they are a thousand times tastier. After that, we wandered through Salzburg at night until 4 a.m.; by then we stumbled upon a 24-hour gas station with a restroom, coffee, etc., which felt like an oasis in the desert for us.
