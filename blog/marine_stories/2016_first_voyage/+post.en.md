My first sea voyage was as a cadet on the ship Ocean Dream (A8WO3).

A cadet is a training position when you work 4 hours as a sailor, and for another 4 hours you keep watch with the assistant captain and absorb his experience. Although it also strongly depends on the company you get into, in some it may be a permanent watch, and in some it may be constant work with sailors, and training is on your own initiative in your free time.
There is no responsibility, but the salary is minimal, at the level of 300-800 dollars a month.
