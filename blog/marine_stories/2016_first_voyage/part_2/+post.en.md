_2016.09.27-28_ There was an incredible typhoon near Taiwan, so we hid near this island and waited for the weather to improve. Our ship rocked about 20 degrees, but at the same time we, the cadets, kept watch on the deck, because mooring rope theft happens in this region.

_2016.10.06_ We got through the storm to Singapore in order to get fuel and provisions. Since a new captain came to us during the repair, he approved the request for provisions by adding a bunch of milk and cookies. He himself was rather obese, but a kind-hearted man.
