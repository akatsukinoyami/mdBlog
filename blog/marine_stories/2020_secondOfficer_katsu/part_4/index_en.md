_2020.11.16 00:30_ I am reporting yesterday.
At 11 o'clock I woke up and wanted to go to the shower, but I heard the knock of 3RMB and the cherished words "Katsu, PSK". (Ship Inspection)
I go up to the bridge, the inspector asked for a folder with certificates and began to check everything.
I did not find one addition to the certificate, and the copy I made did not suit him. (**UPD:** again, ship's certificates are the responsibility of 3PKM, transferred to me)
We descended from the bridge (**UPD:** I was entrusted with the honor of escorting the inspector)

* They started the boats (he did not give time to start the engine and said that one of the two batteries was not working).
* Climbed inside the garbage kommanta and said that there was a lot of garbage. (Although we burned the garbage before arriving at the port, we have been standing here for quite a long time).
* Brought emergency diesel generator.
* Then we went to the CPU (control room for all mechanisms in the engine room)
* She started clicking computers and photographing errors on them.
* Then he told the electrician to open the main switchboard for 220v and 440v, and after digging for a long time, he found an unconnected wire in one, and a luridly soldered resistance in the other.
* Then he went all over the car, looking into every crack and said that the car was dirty and smudged with oil.

When we got back to the bridge, I found that in that addition to the certificate there was a postscript that it was electronic and it failed.
And so he began to show the photo master and tell that there were a lot of comments on the detention of the ship, and the captain almost had to beg him not to do this. Let's see how it goes on.

2020.11.27 02:50
When we entered the port, the border guards called us and told us not to enter a special closed area, and recommended to go a different course.
Moreover, the closure of this area is not indicated in any NAVAREA messages or messages from NAVTEX.
They said something about their PRIPs / NAVIPs, but who is receiving them now, except for the Russian fleet?
Yes, and except for the Internet, which is not, I do not know about the ways to get them.
So, the border guards told our ship to go aside in Russian and we left on the recommended course in accordance with their requirements.
The immigration officer said that we entered the restricted area just a little, but they would turn a blind eye to this.
However, the border guards could not explain to ships with English-speaking crews the need not to enter the closed area, so these ships will now be fined 300k rubles (about 3.5-4k dollars).
For what? Yes, simply because they can.
Russia is a generous soul.
