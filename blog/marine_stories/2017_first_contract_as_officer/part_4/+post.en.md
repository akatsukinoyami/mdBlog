_2018.03.02_ A new chef will arrive around noon today.
Yesterday was not a good day since midnight, first anchoring at 2 am, then at 6 am was called to the bridge to collect ship's certificates for the agent. Then we weighed anchor and proceeded to another anchorage to... Stay there for 10 minutes and take another pilot! Then a crazy mooring, tugboats behaved like crazy, turned us around in a tiny harbor, the size of 1.5-2 of our ships at crazy speed, the towing lines cracked like crazy. Then a lot of delays with unloading, cargo documents, and now, presumably, they want to remoor us astern to the pier. In short, the Arabs have robbed us all of the brains so that no one on the ship understands anything, although we are only the second day in the port. Now we are waiting for at least some information, whether they will re-moor us or not, when the unloading will continue, we ourselves don’t know anything at all (

After that, they again pulled forward, and loaded 6 more blades onto the covers of the holds.
